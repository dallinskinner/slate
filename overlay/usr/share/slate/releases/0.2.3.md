## 0.2.3
- The welcome works with the keyboard: Tab and the arrow keys move, Enter and Space choose.
- Small updates like this one now show a short note instead of the whole welcome.
- New `slate doctor` command: saves a troubleshooting report you can paste into a message.
- No more "Failed to download metadata for LVFS" in Discover on Macs.
- The camera step is skipped when the camera is already set up.
